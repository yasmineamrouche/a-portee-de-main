About

The project

Many specialized domestic violence resources exist in Québec. Yet they are not always known to the people who could benefit from them, especially when asking for help is difficult or risky.

Within Reach aims to lower this barrier by making existing resources visible in everyday spaces.

  • Posters placed in public spaces, such as women's washrooms
  • A website that brings the resources together
  • A QR code that opens the directory
  • A collaboration with community organizations and support workers

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and collects no personal information. To evaluate the project, we anonymously count visits and some clicks (for example on a phone number) using GoatCounter. These numbers cannot identify anyone.
