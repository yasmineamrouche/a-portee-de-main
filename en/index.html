Clear your browsing history

Chrome on Android
Tap ⋮ → Delete browsing data (or ⋮ → History).
Safari on iPhone
Open Settings → Safari (or Settings → Apps → Safari) → Clear History and Website Data.
Computer (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete (on Mac: ⌘ + Shift + Delete).

If clearing all your history might draw attention, delete only the pages from this site. Private browsing does not save your history.

Looking for support?

You are not alone.

Free and confidential resources are available for people experiencing domestic violence, control, threats, or any situation that makes them feel uncomfortable or unsafe.

You can find help, at your own pace.

By phone

Talk to someone now

1 800 363-9010
  • 24/7
  • Free
  • Anonymous and confidential
  • English and French

SOS violence conjugale. A worker will listen, help you assess your safety, and can call a shelter for you. You don't need to be sure it's violence to call.

Sexual violence: Sexual Violence Helpline (Info-aide violence sexuelle), 1 888 933-9007.

Are you in immediate danger?

911

Call 911 (police, ambulance). If you can't call, ask someone you trust to call for you.

Browse safely

If you think your phone, computer or browsing history may be monitored, use a device that person can't access whenever possible. You can also clear your browsing history: tap ⋮ → Delete browsing data. The “Clear browsing history” button at the top of every page shows the steps for your device.
