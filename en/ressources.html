I want to talk to someone

Free, confidential support lines. You can call just to talk, ask questions or be pointed in the right direction.

  • SOS violence conjugale

    24/7

    Phone support, immediate intervention and referrals to shelters. A worker can call a shelter for you. Free, bilingual, anonymous and confidential help by phone, text, chat and email.

    • Phone1 800 363-9010
    • Text438 601-1211
    • Emailsos@sosviolenceconjugale.ca

    Website : sosviolenceconjugale.ca

  • La Dauphinelle

    24/7

    Emergency and transitional housing, post-shelter follow-up and outreach services (listening and counselling) for women and children.

    • Phone514 598-7779

    Website : ladauphinelle.org

  • Refuge pour les femmes de l'Ouest-de-l'Île

    24/7

    24/7 crisis line, emergency and second-stage housing, outreach services and support groups, including for loved ones.

    • Phone514 620-4845 ext. 221 · crisis line

    Website : wiws.ca

  • CAVAC – Centre d'aide aux victimes d'actes criminels

    Support, post-traumatic and psychosocial intervention, information about the court process and support groups. You do not need to file a complaint to get help.

    • Phone1 866 532-2822

    Website : cavac.qc.ca

  • Centre d'amitié autochtone de Montréal

    Emotional support workers for individuals and families affected by the crisis of Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women, Girls and 2SLGBTQIA+ people.

    Website : nfcm.org

  • Info-aide violence sexuelle

    A safe, non-judgmental space where anyone affected by sexual violence is welcomed, believed and supported in their own choices.

    • Phone1 888 933-9007
    • Phone514 933-9007 Montréal

    Website : infoaideviolencesexuelle.ca

I need a safe place to stay

Shelters welcome women, with or without children, free of charge. SOS violence conjugale can check available spaces for you.

    24/7

    Phone support, immediate intervention and referrals to shelters. A worker can call a shelter for you. Free, bilingual, anonymous and confidential help by phone, text, chat and email.

    • Phone1 800 363-9010
    • Text438 601-1211
    • Emailsos@sosviolenceconjugale.ca

    Website : sosviolenceconjugale.ca

  • Auberge Shalom pour femmes

    24/7

    Safe 24/7 shelter, crisis intervention, psychosocial support, services for children, legal and social support. Services adapted to the Jewish community, among others.

    Website : fr.aubergeshalom.org

  • Auberge Transition

    24/7

    Safe housing, psychosocial support, information, referrals and follow-up for women and children experiencing domestic violence.

    • Phone514 481-0495

    Website : aubergetransition.org

  • L'Escale pour Elle

    24/7

    Housing and support for women and children experiencing domestic violence, for their safety and to regain independence.

    • Phone514 493-4004

  • La Dauphinelle

    24/7

    Emergency and transitional housing, post-shelter follow-up and outreach services (listening and counselling) for women and children.

    Website : ladauphinelle.org

    24/7

  • Maison secours aux femmes

I have questions about my relationship

Not sure if what you're experiencing is violence? These tools and lines can help you see more clearly.

  • SOS violence conjugale

    24/7

    Phone support, immediate intervention and referrals to shelters. A worker can call a shelter for you. Free, bilingual, anonymous and confidential help by phone, text, chat and email.

    • Phone1 800 363-9010
    • Text438 601-1211
    • Emailsos@sosviolenceconjugale.ca

    Website : sosviolenceconjugale.ca

  • 211 Québec

    Online directory of community services: domestic violence shelters and support, food aid, immigration and more.

    Website : 211qc.ca

  • Conseil du statut de la femme – organismes ressources

    List of regional and national women's organizations offering support in many areas: work, family, health, violence, immigration.

    Website : csf.gouv.qc.ca

  • Le violentomètre

    Tool

    A simple tool to “measure” whether your relationship is based on consent and respect, or whether it involves violence. (French PDF)

    Website : santemonteregie.qc.ca
